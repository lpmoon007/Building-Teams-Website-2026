The program

Care Cart: the children’s hospital build.

Teams build and stock mobile carts with books, games and toys, decorating them for a child’s bedside. Partway through, they learn the carts are headed to a children’s hospital ward — and the build turns personal. The carts are delivered to the hospital chosen with you.

How a children’s hospital give-back works

  1. Choose the hospital. You pick the cause with us; Legendary Team Building sources, vets and confirms the recipient children’s hospital or ward before the event.
  2. Build the carts. Teams build, stock and decorate mobile carts of books, games and toys over at least two hours.
  3. The reveal. Teams learn the carts are bound for children facing illness and long hospital stays.
  4. The delivery. The finished carts are delivered to the hospital to brighten the ward.
  5. Report the impact. You receive participation numbers and donation totals for CSR and ESG reporting.
The reason it sticks

Your people will remember the ward longer than any slide.

That’s the point. Connect your team to children who can’t be in the room and the bond — and the lesson — lasts. We’ve run give-back events since 2003 and produced the Guinness World Record–setting charity bike build for our client DaVita.

Children’s hospital give-back FAQs

What is children’s hospital team building? +

A corporate give-back where your team builds and stocks mobile carts of books, games and toys that are donated to a children’s hospital ward. Legendary Team Building’s program is Care Cart, for any group size, and the carts are delivered to brighten the days of children facing illness and long stays.

What’s in a Care Cart? +

Each cart is a mobile cart stocked with books, games, toys and comforts and decorated by your team for a child’s bedside. The contents are included; you bring the room and your people.

How big can a Care Cart event be? +

Any group size. Care Cart needs at least two hours, and more people simply means more carts for the hospital ward.

How much does a children’s hospital team building event cost? +

Legendary Team Building’s give-back programs run $50 to $225 per person, including facilitation, all materials and cart contents, the donation, and coordinating delivery to the hospital. Groups over 1,000 are custom-priced.

Which hospital receives the carts? +

A children’s hospital or ward chosen with you and confirmed by Legendary Team Building before the event. The carts are delivered after the build.

Is there a give-back for kids that includes a bike or STEM activity? +

Yes — Legendary Team Building also runs charity bike builds (Building a Dream) and a STEM give-back, Inspiring Minds, for children.

Can a Care Cart build support our CSR and ESG reporting? +

Yes. Participation numbers and donation totals, packaged into an ESG-ready impact report.
