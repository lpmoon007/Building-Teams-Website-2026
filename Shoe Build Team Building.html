What it is

Sole Purpose, in a nutshell.

Sole Purpose pairs an eye-opening look at what it means to go without with the hands-on work of assembling sandals from recycled materials — real shoes that, through Soles4Souls, reach children who have never owned a pair.

How it works

Three moves from empathy to impact.

1

See it through their eyes

The room opens with customizable, experiential content and a short film from Wayne Elsey, founder of Soles4Souls — a grounded look at what going without shoes really means.

2

Build the sandals

At rounds of eight to ten, teams assemble durable sandals from recycled materials — usually two people to a pair, each building a single shoe. Optional Glass Walk and Trap Walk challenges raise the stakes.

3

Send them where they’re needed

Through Soles4Souls, the finished sandals reach children in developing countries — where a pair of shoes can be what lets a child attend school. Then the team returns to their seats for the wrap-up.

What your team takes back

It looks like a shoe build. It debriefs like empathy in practice.

The sandals are the story; the wrap-up is where it turns into behavior. Facilitators tie the experience to the themes your team is working on — here are the three we return to most.

Walk a mile

Ever walk a mile in someone else’s shoes? What if they had none at all? Empathy stops being an abstraction the moment you build a shoe for a child who has never owned one.

Perspective & understanding

Putting yourself in another’s shoes — a teammate’s, a customer’s — is a fundamental building block of every relationship. We crystallize it before your team ever picks up a tool.

One small act

A single pair of shoes can change a person’s life. Small, deliberate acts compound — the same is true of the work your team does every day.
The reason it sticks

Your team builds something real — for a child who needs it.

Every pair reaches a child through Soles4Souls, and because the sandals are built from recycled materials, your team also keeps that material out of the landfill. It’s measurable impact your CSR team can report — and, optionally, teams can sponsor a Shoe Bank, so as a child outgrows one pair they turn it in for the next size, all the way through graduation.

The need is real: an estimated 300 million children worldwide don’t own a pair of shoes — and in many places a child needs shoes to be allowed to attend school.Figure per Soles4Souls, Legendary Team Building’ shoe-build charity partner.

Questions, answered

Shoes That Matter FAQs

What is the Sole Purpose activity? +

Sole Purpose is a shoe build team building activity. Teams assemble durable sandals from recycled materials that, through our partner Soles4Souls, reach children in developing countries who have never owned a pair — where a pair of shoes can be what lets a child attend school.

Who receives the shoes, and who is your charity partner? +

The sandals are distributed through Soles4Souls to children in developing countries. Legendary Team Building runs the experience; Soles4Souls handles distribution.

Can this support CSR reporting? +

Yes. We provide participation numbers and donation totals so your team development doubles as documented community impact.

How many people can take part? +

From 5 to 5,000, including conference general sessions. It works at any group size, seated in rounds of eight to ten.

How long does it take? +

Most sessions run 90 minutes to three hours, and it can anchor a conference theme. We tailor the length to your agenda.

What is a Shoe Bank? +

An optional add-on some organizations sponsor: when a child outgrows a pair of shoes, they turn in the old pair and receive the next size — again and again, all the way through graduation, so there’s always a pair waiting.

What are the Glass Walk and Trap Walk? +

Optional challenges we can layer into the experience to raise the stakes and deepen the team dynamic. Availability depends on your venue.

What’s included? +

Everything to run it end to end: a lead facilitator and event crew, all build materials and kits, the charitable donation of what your team builds, plus full setup and teardown. You bring the room and your people; we bring the rest.

How much does it cost? +

Pricing depends on your group size and city, and it’s set per participant with the charitable donation included. Tell us your group and we’ll send a tailored proposal, usually within a business day.
