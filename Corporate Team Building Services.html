What does a corporate team building company do?

A corporate team building company designs and runs facilitated experiences that improve how a team works — planning, materials, staffing, facilitation and follow-up. The best ones match the format to your goal, scale from a single team to thousands, and tie the day back to real work with measurement. Legendary Team Building does all of that — and most of what we run also produces a donated result for kids in need.

Pricing

What drives the cost.

There’s no flat rate — team building services are priced to the engagement. Five things move the number:

  • ✓Group size — from a team of 5 to a general session of 5,000.
  • ✓Format — a 90-minute build vs. a multi-day offsite or an ongoing program.
  • ✓Donated product — give-back builds include the cost of the bikes, shoes or kits produced.
  • ✓Location & logistics — travel, venue, staging and materials shipping.
  • ✓Measurement & follow-up — optional baselines, 30-day tracking and reporting.

For a deeper breakdown see our corporate team building cost guide — or tell us your parameters and we’ll send a proposal.

Questions, answered

Corporate team building services FAQs

What does a corporate team building company do? +

Designs and runs facilitated experiences that improve how a team works — planning, materials, staffing, facilitation and follow-up. The best ones match the format to your goal, scale from a single team to thousands, and tie the day back to real work with measurement. Most of what we run also produces a donated result for kids in need.

How do I choose a corporate team building company? +

Match the provider to your stakes: a light activity vendor for a low-stakes social; a facilitator or full-service company when the day must change behavior; a CSR provider to add giving; an OD consultancy for multi-year transformation. Legendary Team Building is the full-service, impact-anchored option — measurable outcomes, give-back built in, scale to 5,000. See our honest guide to choosing.

What team building services do you offer? +

Give-back and charity builds, corporate team building events, conference and large-group sessions, workshops, executive team building and the Team LFS leadership simulation, virtual and hybrid formats, team coaching, and ongoing programs — all planned, staffed and facilitated by us.

How much do corporate team building services cost? +

It varies by format, group size, location and length; give-back builds also include the cost of the donated product. Tell us your headcount, goal and budget and we’ll design something that fits — request a proposal for pricing.

Can you run a session that breaks down silos between corporate and field teams? +

Yes — it’s one of the most common reasons we’re hired. HQ and field (or frontline) teams often operate as separate worlds. A shared give-back build puts them at the same table working toward one goal that belongs to neither side, and the facilitated debrief surfaces where the real friction is — so people leave seeing one team, not two camps.

What size groups can you handle? +

From a leadership team of 5 to a conference general session of 5,000. Our formats are engineered to scale so everyone participates at once — we produced the Guinness World Record bike build for DaVita with 5,000 people.

Do you offer virtual and hybrid team building services? +

Yes — fully virtual and hybrid formats, from give-back builds over video to multi-week TeamwoRx programs, with a facilitator in every breakout so remote and in-office people share one experience.

How hands-on are you with pre-event planning and logistics? +

Fully hands-on. We handle materials, staging, shipping, charity-partner coordination and on-site production, and we slot cleanly into your run-of-show — you provide the room and your people; we manage the rest end to end.

Can we see a sample agenda or proposal before we commit? +

Yes. After a short discovery call we send a tailored proposal — a sample run-of-show, group-size and format options, and transparent per-participant pricing with the charitable donation included — usually within one business day, with no obligation.

Do you offer a pre-event discovery call to tailor the design to our objectives? +

Yes. Every engagement starts with a discovery call — no obligation — where we learn your group, goals, meeting theme and the outcome you want (service, quality, trust, leadership or change), then design and price the right experience around it. We reply within one business day. Book a call to start.
