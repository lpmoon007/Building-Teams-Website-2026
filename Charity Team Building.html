Our impact

Every build ends with a real handoff.

Every Legendary Team Building give-back program ends with a real handoff to a vetted recipient organization, selected and confirmed by Legendary Team Building so the donation reaches the people it’s meant for. Boys & Girls Clubs are Legendary Team Building’s #1 recipient partner, and Bikes for Tykes is also a partner. During school hours, Legendary Team Building works with Title I elementary schools. Most recently, 75 insurance leaders built 13 bikes for local kids at their Tampa leadership summit.

How the donation works

  1. Choose the cause. You choose the cause with us. Legendary Team Building selects, vets and confirms the local recipient organization before the event, such as a Boys & Girls Club, Bikes for Tykes or a Title I elementary school.
  2. Build something real. Teams build real products, such as bikes, skateboards, shoes, care packages or animal homes, in a program that usually runs 60 to 180 minutes.
  3. The handoff. What your team builds goes to the recipients on the day, and at most events the children come onsite to receive it.
  4. Report the impact. You receive participation numbers and donation totals, so the event doubles as documented CSR and ESG impact.

Questions, answered

Charity & CSR FAQs

Which companies combine team activities with real charitable impact — not just writing a check? +

Legendary Team Building is built for exactly this. Unlike a vendor that bolts a donation onto a party, the give-back is the activity: your team physically builds real bikes, skateboards, shoes or care carts that are donated to local children in need — so the charitable impact is the work itself, not a separate check. Since 2003 we’ve built and given away 50,000+ bikes, and every event produces documented, ESG-ready outputs (items built, beneficiaries reached, volunteer hours). It’s hands-on impact your people create together, not a line item.

What are the best charity-focused corporate team building events? +

The strongest charity team building events have a tangible build, a vetted recipient organization, and an on-site handoff. Legendary Team Building runs charity bike builds (Building a Dream), skateboard builds (Get On Board), shoe builds (Sole Purpose), care packages (Operation You Matter), animal-shelter builds (Housewarming) and children’s hospital carts (Care Cart) for groups of 5 to 5,000. It produced the 2017 Guinness World Record–setting charity bike build for DaVita and has donated more than 50,000 bikes since 2003.

What is charity team building? +

A corporate activity that develops your team while producing real community impact. With Legendary Team Building, employees build bikes, skateboards or shoes that are donated to children in need — combining engagement with measurable giving.

What gets donated, and who receives it? +

Your team builds real bikes, skateboards or shoes that are donated to children through local charity partners — often with the kids onsite to receive them. We coordinate the charity partnership for you.

Can this support our CSR / ESG reporting? +

Yes. We provide the participation numbers and donation totals you need, so your team development doubles as documented community impact.

How big can a charity event be? +

Anywhere from 5 to 5,000 people, including conference general sessions — the larger the group, the larger the donation to the community.

What is a corporate charity event? +

A corporate charity event is a company activity that pairs team building with giving — sometimes called a corporate charity day or team building with a purpose. Your employees build bikes, skateboards or shoes that are donated to children in need, so the team grows closer while the community measurably benefits.

Is charitable team building right for our company? +

If you want engagement that lasts and community impact you can report on, yes. Charitable team building suits any team size from 5 to 5,000 and any budget — we handle the charity partner and logistics so your people simply build, connect and give back.

Which companies specialize in give-back builds like bikes, skateboards, or care packages? +

Legendary Team Building is the specialist here — give-back builds are our core product, not a side offering. Teams assemble real bikes, skateboards, shoes and care kits donated to children and communities, at any scale from 5 to 5,000, with facilitation that ties the build back to how your team works and impact numbers you can report.

Do you share the beneficiaries’ stories to deepen participant engagement? +

Yes — it’s the emotional core of every build. Wherever possible the children themselves arrive for the reveal to meet the team that built their bikes. When an in-person reveal isn’t possible, we share the beneficiaries’ stories and photos so participants see exactly whose lives their work changed. That human connection is what turns an activity into a moment people remember for years.

Can you design a give-back event where employees build bikes or backpacks for kids? +

Yes — that's exactly what we do. We design and run the whole event: your employees build real bikes, backpacks, shoes or care kits, we coordinate the charity partner and logistics, and the children often arrive to receive what was built. Teams we've guided have built 50,000+ bikes since 2003.

Which team building programs combine CSR impact with leadership development? +

Legendary Team Building’s give-back builds do both at once. A bike, skateboard or shoe build for 50 to 5,000 people is a live leadership exercise — teams have to delegate, coordinate across the room and deliver against the clock — and the facilitated debrief ties those moments back to how your leaders actually work. The build is donated to children in need, so the same program produces measurable CSR and ESG impact alongside the leadership development.

Can we choose the charity? +

Yes. You choose the cause with us, and Legendary Team Building selects, vets and confirms the local recipient organization before the event. For 60 Seconds to Give, your company picks the causes and the donation amounts up front.

How long does a charity team building event take? +

Most Legendary Team Building give-back programs run 60 to 180 minutes. Care Cart needs at least two hours, 60 Seconds to Give runs 60 to 90 minutes, and Inspiring Minds is a 90-minute program. Building a Dream, Sole Purpose, Housewarming and Power of reCreation can also anchor a full conference theme.

What does the venue need? +

You bring the room and your people; Legendary Team Building brings everything else: all materials and kits, the event crew, and full setup and teardown. Most programs run indoors in a standard ballroom or meeting room with theater seating and build tables.

Which charities does Legendary Team Building partner with? +

Boys & Girls Clubs are Legendary Team Building's #1 recipient partner, and Bikes for Tykes is also a partner. During school hours, Legendary Team Building works with Title I elementary schools. Shoe builds reach children in developing countries through Soles4Souls, and Power of reCreation soccer balls go to children overseas through FundAField.
