Team building statistics (2026)

StatisticsUpdated August 2026Legendary Team Building proprietary data + cited research
The short version

Across 25,000+ participant evaluations, Legendary Team Building averages 4.76 out of 5 on “This activity was a good use of my time.” 35% of clients rebook and 5% of those return 10+ times. Since 2003 the company has run 2,000+ events for 1,500+ companies and 500,000+ participants, and built and donated 50,000+ bikes to children. Meanwhile, industry-wide, employee engagement sits at a record-low 20% (Gallup) — the gap these numbers are meant to close.

Team building statistics FAQs

What is Legendary Team Building’ average participant rating? +

Legendary Team Building averages 4.76 out of 5 on the statement “This activity was a good use of my time” across more than 25,000 participant evaluations. At DaVita’s 2,200-person national meeting the same question scored 4.88 out of 5.

What percentage of clients rebook Legendary Team Building? +

35% of clients book again, and 5% of those repeat clients return 10 or more times — an unusually strong repeat rate for corporate team building.

How many bikes has Legendary Team Building donated? +

More than 50,000 bikes have been built at Legendary Team Building events and donated to children in need since 2003, tracked through the company’s accounting records.

What is the state of employee engagement in 2026? +

Gallup’s 2026 State of the Global Workplace report found global employee engagement fell to 20% in 2025 — its lowest since 2020 and down from a 23% peak in 2022 — with low engagement costing the global economy about $10 trillion a year, roughly 9% of GDP.
