How it works

Three moves from resistance to “let’s go.”

1

Meet change head-on

We open by naming the real problem: change we don’t want is what feels hard. Then children arrive — one to a team — to help the adults through what comes next.

2

The hilarious point-chase

Teams earn points in a fast, funny, task-driven event. When the tasks are done, the boards come out: a fingerboard for every adult to assemble, and a full skateboard for every child.

3

The 180° shift

Somewhere in it, people flip — from resisting change to driving it. Each child rides away with a custom skateboard, helmet, backpack and pads, and your team carries the shift back to the launch or initiative that needs it.

Questions, answered

The Power of Change FAQs

What is the Get On Board activity? +

Get On Board (The Power of Change) is a corporate team building activity about embracing change. Children arrive onsite, one to a team, and adults assemble fingerboards through a hilarious task-driven event; each child receives a full custom skateboard, helmet, backpack and pads. It’s designed to turn resistance to change into buy-in.

What is the takeaway for my team? +

A genuine shift in how people respond to change — plus stronger communication and resilience, anchored by the emotion of giving the boards to kids.

How many people can take part? +

From 5 to unlimited, including conference general sessions. The more participants, the more impact — and the more people who get on board.

How long does it take? +

75 minutes to three hours; 75 to 120 minutes is the common range. We tailor the length to your agenda.

Who gets the skateboards? +

Both adults and children. Each adult assembles a small fingerboard, and each child rides away with a full-size custom skateboard, helmet, backpack, and knee and elbow pads.

Are children really part of the event? +

Yes. Children arrive onsite and join the teams — one child to a team — to help the adults through the experience. It's what makes the change lesson land so hard.

What is Get On Board best for? +

Any time you need people to willingly embrace change — a product launch, a policy change, or a new initiative. It's designed to turn resistance into buy-in.

What’s included? +

Everything to run it end to end: a lead facilitator and event crew, all build materials and kits, the charitable donation of what your team builds, plus full setup and teardown. You bring the room and your people; we bring the rest.

How much does it cost? +

Pricing depends on your group size and city, and it’s set per participant with the charitable donation included. Tell us your group and we’ll send a tailored proposal, usually within a business day.
