DaVitaFortune 500 · Kidney care · Dallas, 2005

2,200 people. Four rooms. One reveal.

Updated August 2026

In 2005, in Dallas, we produced what is still the most logistically demanding build we’ve ever run: 2,200 DaVita teammates spread across four airwall-divided rooms, four speakers, and a single reveal that had to land at the exact same second in every room.

In short

For DaVita’s 2005 national meeting in Dallas, Building Teams ran a 2,200-person bike build across four rooms divided by airwalls. Because no single room could hold everyone, the hardest part was the reveal: 400 children had to enter all four rooms at the same instant, cued by four DaVita speakers working in perfect sync — so no room saw it coming first. It landed, and the event was rated 4.88 out of 5.

The challenge

DaVita wanted its 2,200 teammates to share one emotional high point at a high-stakes national meeting. But 2,200 people don’t fit in one room — the venue split them across four separate spaces divided by airwalls, each with its own stage and speaker.

That created the real problem. The magic of a give-back build is the reveal — the moment the children walk in to meet the teams who just built their bikes. With four rooms, that moment had to happen in all four at once. If one room saw the kids first, word would travel through the airwalls and the surprise would collapse for everyone else.

What we did

We designed and produced a synchronized four-room build — staging, timing and facilitation choreographed so all four rooms moved through the build on the same clock and finished together.

The centerpiece was the reveal. We scripted and cued four DaVita speakers, one per room, to a shared countdown, so 400 children entered every room on the identical beat. Four rooms, four stages, four speakers — one perfectly synchronized moment.

The reveal

3… 2… 1 — doors open. In all four rooms at once, 400 children streamed in to meet the teammates who had just built their bikes. Nobody saw it coming, and everybody saw it together.

The impact

Four hundred children received a brand-new bike. Twenty-two hundred DaVita teammates shared a single moment they’d retell for years — and the event scored a 4.88 out of 5, one of the highest ratings we’ve ever recorded at scale.

Why it worked

Big without the chaos.

An event this size only lands if the logistics are invisible and the reveal is flawless. Running four rooms as one event — and timing the reveal to the second across four speakers — is exactly the production rigor that separates a memorable moment from a scramble.

  • ✓Synchronized at scale — four rooms on one clock, one shared reveal.
  • ✓Everyone participates — no rotations, no one watching from the side.
  • ✓Built-in purpose — 400 children met the teams who built their bikes.

Twelve years later we took the same discipline even bigger — a Guinness World Record build for 5,000 DaVita teammates in Denver.
